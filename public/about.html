Who We Are

Graceland

Registered Private Voluntary Organisation (PVO 18/26) • Zimbabwe • Restoring Hope, Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives

Company Profile

Restoring Hope, Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives

GRACELAND is a humanitarian and community development organisation committed to improving the quality of life of vulnerable individuals and communities in need of rehabilitation support across Zimbabwe.

We work with disadvantaged children, persons with disabilities, vulnerable families, the elderly, and people affected by drug and substance abuse, offering care, support, and rehabilitation opportunities.

Through compassionate service and sustainable development initiatives, Graceland seeks to restore dignity, create opportunities, and promote rehabilitation, self-reliance, and social inclusion among vulnerable populations.

Legal Status: Graceland is a duly registered Private Voluntary Organisation (PVO 18/26) operating in Zimbabwe in terms of the Private Voluntary Organisations Act [Chapter 17:05].

The organisation is authorised to carry out charitable, welfare, educational, rehabilitation, healthcare, and community development activities in accordance with its approved registration objectives.

Our focus is on rehabilitation, community support, child protection, disability inclusion, healthcare access, and empowerment.

About Graceland

Graceland is a humanitarian and community development organisation committed to improving the quality of life of vulnerable individuals and communities in need of rehabilitation support across Zimbabwe.

The organisation works with disadvantaged children, persons with disabilities, vulnerable families, the elderly, and people affected by drug and substance abuse, offering care, support, and rehabilitation opportunities.

Through compassionate service and sustainable development initiatives, Graceland seeks to restore dignity, create opportunities, and promote rehabilitation, self-reliance, and social inclusion among vulnerable populations.

Vision

To build inclusive, empowered, and self-reliant communities where every vulnerable person has access to opportunities, healthcare, education, protection, and a dignified life.

Mission

To transform lives through compassionate social support, education, healthcare, rehabilitation, and sustainable community empowerment programmes.

Registration Objectives

  • Provide welfare and charitable assistance to vulnerable individuals and communities.
  • Offer educational support and care services to disadvantaged children and vulnerable groups.
  • Facilitate healthcare access, rehabilitation services, and health support programmes.
  • Empower vulnerable individuals through life-skills development and sustainable livelihood initiatives.
  • Establish and support residential care facilities, children’s homes, rehabilitation centres, and community health facilities.

Core Values

  • Compassion
  • Integrity
  • Accountability
  • Inclusivity
  • Transparency
  • Respect for Human Dignity
  • Community Empowerment
  • Excellence in Service

Core Programmes

  1. Community Welfare Support: Provision of food assistance, clothing, household essentials, emergency relief, and social support to vulnerable households.
  2. Child Protection and Care: Educational assistance, nutrition support, psychosocial care, protection services, and safe care initiatives for vulnerable and orphaned children.
  3. Disability Support Services: Promotion of inclusion through mobility aids, assistive devices, healthcare referrals, rehabilitation support, and advocacy for persons with disabilities.
  4. Healthcare Access and Rehabilitation: Facilitating access to medical care, rehabilitation services, health awareness campaigns, and community health support programmes.
  5. Drug and Substance Rehabilitation: Prevention awareness, counselling support, rehabilitation referrals, psychosocial support, family reintegration, and community-based recovery programmes.
  6. Community Empowerment: Life-skills training, entrepreneurship development, vocational support, and income-generating initiatives that promote self-reliance and economic resilience.
  7. Residential Care and Rehabilitation: Support for children’s homes, rehabilitation centres, residential care facilities, and community health institutions serving vulnerable populations.

Our Impact

Graceland has contributed to positive change through:

  • Partnerships that strengthen sustainable community development across Zimbabwe.
