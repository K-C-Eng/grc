About Graceland PVO

Registered, community-centred and committed to restoring hope

Driven by compassion, dignity, and service, Graceland works closely with disadvantaged children, persons with disabilities, vulnerable families, the elderly, and underserved communities to restore hope and create opportunities for a better future.

  • Legal status: Registered Private Voluntary Organisation (PVO 18/26).
  • Vision: To build inclusive, empowered, and self-reliant communities where every vulnerable individual has access to opportunities, quality healthcare, education, and a dignified life.
  • Mission: To improve the lives of vulnerable children, families, persons with disabilities, and disadvantaged communities through sustainable social support, education, healthcare, rehabilitation, and community empowerment initiatives.
  • Objectives: To offer inclusive education and support, empower disadvantaged individuals, support residential care and rehabilitation facilities, and facilitate access to quality healthcare services.
  • Core values: Compassion, integrity, accountability, inclusivity, respect for human dignity, transparency, community empowerment, and excellence in service.

Our Impact

Graceland has positively impacted vulnerable communities through food and nutritional support, assistance to children living with disabilities, community outreach programmes, support for vulnerable households, promotion of social inclusion and dignity for persons with disabilities, and building partnerships that strengthen community development.

Partnership Approach

Graceland believes sustainable development is achieved through collaboration. We welcome partnerships with government ministries, local authorities, corporate organisations, churches, development agencies, international NGOs, community-based organisations, and individual donors and volunteers.
